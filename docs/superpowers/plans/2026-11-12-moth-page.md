- The spec governs all section changes, editorial tone, design and acceptance. Its confident natural history supersedes older caveat-heavy assessments.
- Seasonal patterns pool all available years by default. Individual years are optional comparisons.
- Chart labels and values must be readable at 320px and 390px. Essential values work without hover or color alone.
- Preserve old moth section anchors and seasonal return context. Current weather is independent of historical filters.
- Keep useful estimates and notable finds prominent; do not invent local observations or quantities.
- Edit generator source, never generated public files. No production publication or pushing shared branches.

## Review Focus

- Empty or partly undated records: stable full-year data and useful empty states (Task 1 and Task 2).

- Regional mismatch and fallback: actual overlap and correct geography without denominator clamping (Task 1).

- Old anchors, record drilldowns and stale/no forecast: working navigation and clear planning status (Task 2).
